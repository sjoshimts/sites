System Design & Calibration

Environmental Integration.

Component selection is only the initial phase of deployment. Accurate audio reproduction relies heavily on the physical properties of the room and spatial geometry.

We analyze the acoustic characteristics of your space to specify and implement hardware configurations that perform to reference standards. This includes considerations for structural mounting, concealed acoustic treatments, and precise DSP (Digital Signal Processing) calibration.
