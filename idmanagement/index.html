Bureau Service

My Cards Online

Putting you in control of your cards

My Cards online is a card bureau service that lets you not only print your cards but also manage your cardholders data. Based on the cloud you can access the software from anywhere allowing you to keep track of print jobs, manage who has active cards and send out requests for data. Using a unique login your cardholders can take their own photos and confirm their data, reducing mistakes and allowing you to monitor the progress of your cards.
